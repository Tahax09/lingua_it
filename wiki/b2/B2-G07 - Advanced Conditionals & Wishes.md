# B2-G07 — Advanced Conditionals & Wishes

> Level **B2** · grammar

> [!tip] Quick explanation (English)
> Advanced conditionals: mixed hypotheticals cross time — Se avessi studiato (past), adesso avrei un lavoro migliore (present). Wishes: magari / se solo + imperfect subjunctive for the present (Magari fossi in vacanza!) or pluperfect for the past (Se solo l'avessi saputo!). Vorrei che + imperfect subjunctive: Vorrei che tu venissi.

> [!note] شرح سريع (عربي)
> الشرط المتقدّم: الشرط المختلط يعبُر الأزمنة — Se avessi studiato, adesso avrei un lavoro migliore = لو درستُ لكان عندي الآن عمل أفضل. التمنّي: magari / se solo + congiuntivo imperfetto للحاضر (Magari fossi in vacanza! = ليتني في عطلة) أو trapassato للماضي. Vorrei che + congiuntivo imperfetto: Vorrei che tu venissi.

## Elevated register and advanced conditionals
<!-- src: B2 Part 1 · 1.8 -->

| **Pattern**                      | **Esempio**                                                  | **Notes**                    |
|----------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| qualora + congiuntivo imperfetto | Qualora fosse necessario…                                    | = if / in case; formal       |
| nel caso in cui + congiuntivo    | Nel caso in cui venga…                                       | = in the event that          |
| ove + congiuntivo                | Ove fosse possibile…                                         | = where / if; legal, written |
| laddove                          | Laddove necessario…                                          | = where / in cases where     |
| avendo + participio (gerund)     | Avendo riflettuto a lungo, sono giunto alla conclusione che… | Having… — written            |
| posto che / ammesso che          | Posto che le premesse siano corrette…                        | Given that / assuming that   |

*Note: the «non» in «a meno che non piova» — the «non» is expletive (it does not make the sentence negative); you may omit it. «Ove» is rare in speech but common in legal Italian.*

## Conditionals and wishes — advanced
<!-- src: B2 Part 4 · B2+.3 -->

| **Pattern**                         | **Esempio**                                            |
|-------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Mixed (past cause, present result)  | Se avessi studiato, ora sarei laureato.                |
| Mixed (present cause, past result)  | Se fossi più prudente, non avresti avuto l'incidente.  |
| Wish about present                  | Magari fossi qui! · Vorrei che tu fossi qui.           |
| Wish about past                     | Magari avessi studiato! · Vorrei aver studiato.        |
| Polite / reported                   | Potrebbe aiutarmi? · Ha detto che sarebbe venuto.      |
| Hypothesis with gerund / participle | Volendo, potresti venire. · Visto così, sembra facile. |
| a patto che / purché / nel caso che | Ti presto la macchina purché tu sia prudente.          |

## 🇬🇧🇸🇦 Bilingual lesson — Advanced conditionals & wishes
<!-- src: Bilingual Lessons · B2-G07 -->

### Se l'avessi saputo… — «لو كنتُ أعرف…»
*English:* At B1 you learned the three types of *se*. At B2 you mix them across time, express regrets and wishes, and build hypotheses without *se* at all.
*العربية:* في B1 تعلّمت الأنواع الثلاثة لـ se. في B2 تمزج بينها عبر الزمن، وتعبّر عن الندم والأمنيات، وتبني الافتراض دون se أصلاً.

#### Mixed conditionals · الشرط المختلط
| Italian | Logic | English | العربية |
|---|---|---|---|
| Se avessi studiato medicina, adesso sarei medico. | past condition → present result | If I had studied medicine, I'd be a doctor now. | لو درستُ الطب لكنت الآن طبيباً. |
| Se fossi più organizzato, avrei finito ieri. | present condition → past result | If I were more organised, I'd have finished yesterday. | لو كنت أكثر تنظيماً لأنهيت أمس. |
| Se avrò tempo, verrò. | real future | If I have time, I'll come. | إن كان عندي وقت سآتي. |

#### Wishes and regrets · الأمنيات والندم
| Italian | English | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| Magari fossi in vacanza! | If only I were on holiday! | يا ليتني في عطلة! |
| Magari l'avessi saputo prima! | If only I had known earlier! | يا ليتني عرفت ذلك من قبل! |
| Vorrei che tu fossi qui. | I wish you were here. | أتمنى لو كنت هنا. |
| Avrei voluto che tu venissi. | I would have liked you to come. | كنت أودّ أن تأتي. |
| Mi sarebbe piaciuto vederti. | I would have liked to see you. | كان سيسعدني أن أراك. |

#### Hypotheses without se · افتراض بدون se
| Italian | English | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| Al posto tuo, accetterei. | In your place, I'd accept. | لو كنت مكانك لقبلت. |
| Nel caso che piova, restiamo a casa. | In case it rains, we stay home. | في حال أمطرت نبقى في البيت. |
| Qualora fosse necessario, La contatteremo. | Should it be necessary, we'll contact you. (formal) | إذا اقتضى الأمر سنتواصل معكم. |
| Avendo tempo, studierei di più. | Having time, I'd study more. | لو كان لديّ وقت لدرست أكثر. |

> [!warning] Spoken vs written · الكلام مقابل الكتابة
> **EN:** In casual speech you'll hear *Se lo sapevo, venivo* (imperfetto in both halves). Understand it, but write *Se l'avessi saputo, sarei venuto*.
> **AR:** في الكلام العامي ستسمع Se lo sapevo, venivo (imperfetto في الجزأين). افهمها، لكن اكتب Se l'avessi saputo, sarei venuto.
