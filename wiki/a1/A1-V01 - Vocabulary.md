**1. Numbers & Money**

*Count out loud. Prices: €3,50 = tre euro e cinquanta · €15,99 = quindici euro e novantanove.*

| **Italiano**                   | **English**            | **العربية**               | **Esempio**                                                         |
|--------------------------------|------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **zero**                       | zero                   | صفر                       | *Il mio numero inizia con zero.* / My number starts with zero.      |
| **uno**                        | one                    | واحد                      | *Ho solo un fratello.* / I only have one brother.                   |
| **due**                        | two                    | اثنان                     | *Vorrei due caffè, per favore.* / I would like two coffees, please. |
| **tre**                        | three                  | ثلاثة                     | *Sono le tre del pomeriggio.* / It is three in the afternoon.       |
| **quattro**                    | four                   | أربعة                     | *Abito al numero quattro.* / I live at number four.                 |
| **cinque**                     | five                   | خمسة                      | *Lavoro cinque giorni a settimana.* / I work five days a week.      |
| **sei**                        | six                    | ستة                       | *Ceno alle sei.* / I have dinner at six.                            |
| **sette**                      | seven                  | سبعة                      | *La settimana ha sette giorni.* / The week has seven days.          |
| **otto**                       | eight                  | ثمانية                    | *Dormo otto ore a notte.* / I sleep eight hours a night.            |
| **nove**                       | nine                   | تسعة                      | *Il negozio apre alle nove.* / The store opens at nine.             |
| **dieci**                      | ten                    | عشرة                      | *Costa dieci euro.* / It costs ten euros.                           |
| **venti**                      | twenty                 | عشرون                     | *Costa venti euro.* / It costs twenty euros.                        |
| **trenta**                     | thirty                 | ثلاثون                    | *Ho trentadue anni.* / I am thirty-two years old.                   |
| **cento**                      | one hundred            | مئة                       | *Costa cento euro.* / It costs one hundred euros.                   |
| **Quanto costa?**              | How much does it cost? | كم السعر؟                 |                                                                     |
| **Posso pagare con la carta?** | Can I pay by card?     | هل يمكنني الدفع بالبطاقة؟ |                                                                     |
| **Solo contanti.**             | Cash only.             | نقداً فقط.                 |                                                                     |
| **Il resto, per favore.**      | The change, please.    | الباقي من فضلك.           |                                                                     |
