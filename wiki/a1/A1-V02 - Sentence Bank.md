**Greetings & Introductions**

**Essential Greetings**

| **Italiano** | **English**                                             |
|--------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Ciao!        | Hi/Bye! (Informal, friends)                             |
| Buongiorno!  | Good morning/day! (Formal; morning and early afternoon) |
| Buonasera!   | Good evening! (From mid-afternoon on; varies by region) |
| Buonanotte!  | Good night! (Before sleeping)                           |
| Arrivederci! | Goodbye! (Formal)                                       |
| A presto!    | See you soon!                                           |
| A domani!    | See you tomorrow!                                       |

**Meeting People**

| **Italiano**              | **English**                    |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Come ti chiami?           | What's your name? (informal)   |
| Mi chiamo Marco.          | My name is Marco.              |
| Come si chiama?           | What's your name? (formal)     |
| Piacere di conoscerti!    | Nice to meet you! (informal)   |
| Di dove sei?              | Where are you from? (informal) |
| Sono americano/americana. | I'm American. (m/f)            |
| Abito a Roma.             | I live in Rome.                |
| Quanti anni hai?          | How old are you?               |
| Ho venticinque anni.      | I'm 25 years old.              |
