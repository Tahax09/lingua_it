## Quiz answer, explained
<!-- src: B1 Part 7 · B1.S5 -->

**"Penso che lui _____ ragione."** — Correct answer: **B — abbia**.

- *Penso che* + different subject triggers the subjunctive.
- A — *ha* is indicative and wrong here.
- C — *avere* is the infinitive; it can't follow "che".
- D — *abbi* is the tu imperative (and not a subjunctive form).
- Remember: io/tu/lui all share *abbia* in the present subjunctive.
