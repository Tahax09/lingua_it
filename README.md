## What this is

A complete Italian grammar and practice course from **A1 (beginner)** to **B2 (upper-intermediate)**, written for **Arabic speakers who learn in English**. It is split into 87 short topic pages instead of four very long files:

- **Grammar pages** start with a quick explanation in English and the same explanation in Arabic (شرح سريع), then the full tables and examples.
- **50 lessons** sorted by level in [`wiki/lessons.md`](wiki/lessons.md): 33 written in Arabic (🇸🇦) and 17 bilingual English + Arabic lessons (🇬🇧🇸🇦), so every grammar topic has one. Errors found in the Arabic lessons are fixed and noted on the page.
- **Vocabulary** lists give the Italian word with its English and Arabic meanings, plus sentence banks sorted by situation.
- **Speaking practice** runs 83 days with model answers, alongside dialogues, slang and role-plays.
- **Exercises** come with answer keys: more than 380 extra items plus weekly checkpoints.
- **Revision sheets** for each level include the mistakes Arabic speakers commonly make.

Every topic sits at the level where it is first taught. A1 basics that had been filed in the A2 material were moved back to A1, and the mixed A0 → B1 roadmap was split across the levels it covers.

## How to use it

**On GitHub:** start from [`wiki/lessons.md`](wiki/lessons.md) (lessons in study order) or [`wiki/index.md`](wiki/index.md) (every page), or open a level below.

**In Obsidian (recommended):**

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Tahax09/lingua_it.git
```

Then in Obsidian choose **Open folder as vault** and select `lingua_it`. The `[[links]]` between pages and the graph view will work, and the EN/AR callouts render as coloured boxes.

**Suggested path:**

| You are… | Start here |
|---|---|
| New to Italian | [A1-G01 · Present Tense](wiki/a1/A1-G01%20-%20Present%20Tense%20%E2%80%94%20Regular%20Verbs.md) → then through A1 in order |
| A2, but A1 is fuzzy | The **A1 quick-recall set** in [`wiki/overview.md`](wiki/overview.md) |
| Finishing A2 | [A2-G11 · Tense Clue Words](wiki/a2/A2-G11%20-%20Tense%20Clue%20Words%20%26%20Time%20Structures.md) + [A2-R01 · Revision Sheet](wiki/a2/A2-R01%20-%20One-Page%20Revision%20Sheet.md) |
| Starting B1 | [B1-R03 · A2 → B1 Bridge](wiki/b1/B1-R03%20-%20CEFR%20Can-Do%20%E2%80%94%20B1%20%28A2%20%E2%86%92%20B1%20Bridge%29.md) → [B1-G01 · Congiuntivo](wiki/b1/B1-G01%20-%20Congiuntivo%20Presente%20%E2%80%94%20Forms.md) |

## Example page

> [!tip] Quick explanation (English)
> Passato prossimo = finished action at a specific time in the past. Form: present of avere/essere + participle (-ato, -uto, -ito).

> [!note] شرح سريع (عربي)
> passato prossimo = حدث انتهى في وقت محدّد في الماضي. التكوين: مضارع avere/essere + اسم المفعول.

## Studying with an AI tutor

The vault doubles as a workspace for an LLM tutor (it was built with [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code)). [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) defines the page format, the level-placement rules and these commands:

- `review A1-G04`: a short EN + AR refresher, then a few questions; the page's status is updated
- `quiz me on passato prossimo vs imperfetto`: a graded quiz, saved under `wiki/quizzes/`
- `ingest <file>`: new material is sorted into the right level and pages
- `lint the wiki`: finds misplaced content, broken links and missing Arabic
